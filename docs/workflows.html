Workflows

Brut ships with a small bank of ready-made workflows: chains of nodes already wired, with their prompts and models set, for the jobs that come back every week. You find them in two places: the Workflows row of the home screen, and the Library gallery (the book icon on the left rail, or File ▾ → Browse library…), on the From Brut shelf.

Every card on this page is generated from the same files the app ships, so what you read here is exactly what drops on your canvas.

#How a workflow lands

  • A composite drops as one closed card with a few knobs on its face: a brief, a mode, a shot count, the model of a stage. Wire your image into its inlet, set the knobs, press Run. Open the card (⤢) when you want to see or edit the nodes inside.
  • A template drops onto your canvas too, as a group of live nodes named after it. Nothing is hidden; edit anything. (Nothing on a card starts a new graph: that is the home's own New graph tile.)
  • Every image stage is pinned to a named model at a named size, so a workflow gives the same kind of result on every seat. Video and upscale stages ship unset: the provider choice, and its price, are yours. The card tells you when a stage still needs a pick.
  • Try with sample, on the workflows that ship with one, copies a sample picture into your project and drops the workflow already wired to it, with the shipped result on the card (captioned sample result) so you see what it does before paying for a run. Nothing runs until you press Run; a real run replaces the sample result.
  • A card glows while a stage inside it renders, like any node, and its title bar carries 🕘 (every past take of a stage) and ↻ (a fresh take of a stage); a card with several stages asks which one.
  • Cards whose result is a clip rest on the still they take and play the clip on hover.
  • Once a card has a result, its Next chips offer the workflows that usually follow: one click drops the next card beside it, wired to the result.
  • A dropped card is frozen at the version you dropped: it never changes under you. When Brut ships a newer version of that workflow, the card shows a small vN in the library chip; drop the workflow again to get the new recipe.
  • Your own saved composites and templates sit on the Yours shelf, as the same kind of cards.

The Never pay twice rule holds inside a workflow: a changed knob re-renders the stage it writes to, everything else stays cached.

## Make your own

Any chain you have tuned can become a workflow of your own. Select its nodes, Collapse to composite, and press 💾 Save to library on the card; the save dialog asks for a category from the pick list (Architecture, Characters, Cinema, Text, or one you type) and keeps the card's result as the cover. ⚙ Add knob on the card exposes an inner field on its face. A whole graph saves as a template from File ▾ → Save graph as template…. The details, ports, knobs and versions, are in Library & composites.
